GIMP 3 plug-in · free · bring your own key

Product photo backgrounds, generated inside GIMP

PackshotForge rebuilds the background around your product with Google's Gemini image model, then composites your original product pixels back on top, so the model cannot redraw your label.

Download on GitHub — free, no signup

No pip, no dependencies: copy one folder into GIMP's plug-ins directory. MIT licensed. Your images go directly from your machine to Google's API — nowhere else.

What it does

  • Scene presets for e-commerce product photos, including a pure white marketplace background.
  • Pixel-Lock: your selection of the product is composited back over the generated scene, so label text and logos stay exactly as photographed.
  • Marketplace export sizes built in: Amazon 2000×2000 on white, Etsy 3000×2400, Shopify 2048×2048.

What it costs

The plug-in is free. Image generation is billed by Google per image on your own key — there is no free tier for image generation. You see the estimated cost and confirm before every call.

Requirements

  • GIMP 3.0 or newer (macOS, Linux, Windows)
  • A Google Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com

FAQ

Is there a Photoshop version? Not any more. The Photoshop script was withdrawn on 17 November 2026.

Is this affiliated with Google or the GIMP project? No. PackshotForge is an independent tool that calls Google's public Gemini API with your key.

Do my photos pass through your servers? Never. The plug-in talks directly to Google's API from your machine.
